# AFFILIATE PARTNERSHIP REVIEW CHECKLIST
## Pre-Application Assessment for Travel Comparison Platforms

---

## ✅ LEGITIMACY SIGNALS (What Partners Look For)

### 1. Legal & Compliance Foundation
- [ ] Clear "About Us" page explaining business model
- [ ] Transparent affiliate disclosure (dedicated page)
- [ ] Service disclaimer stating platform doesn't sell tickets
- [ ] Privacy policy (GDPR, CCPA compliant)
- [ ] Terms and conditions
- [ ] Contact information clearly visible
- [ ] Legal entity details (company registration, location)
- [ ] Cookie consent banner (EU/UK)

### 2. Technical Infrastructure
- [ ] Professional domain name (not free hosting)
- [ ] SSL certificate (HTTPS)
- [ ] Fast page load times (<3 seconds)
- [ ] Mobile-responsive design
- [ ] No broken links or 404 errors
- [ ] Clean, professional design
- [ ] Working search functionality
- [ ] Stable uptime (99%+)

### 3. Content Quality
- [ ] Original content (not scraped)
- [ ] Clear, professional writing
- [ ] No spelling/grammar errors
- [ ] Neutral, informational tone (not overly promotional)
- [ ] No false claims ("cheapest prices guaranteed")
- [ ] No airline logos without permission
- [ ] No fake reviews or testimonials
- [ ] Regular content updates

### 4. User Experience
- [ ] Clear value proposition
- [ ] Intuitive navigation
- [ ] Transparent pricing display
- [ ] No deceptive advertising
- [ ] No pop-ups blocking content
- [ ] Clear call-to-action buttons
- [ ] Easy to use search interface
- [ ] Filter and sort functionality

### 5. Transparency Indicators
- [ ] Statement: "We don't sell tickets"
- [ ] Statement: "Bookings completed on partner websites"
- [ ] Statement: "We may earn a commission"
- [ ] Clear indication when leaving site (external link icons)
- [ ] No hidden affiliate links
- [ ] Partner logos/mentions (if applicable)
- [ ] Editorial independence statement

## 🔍 WHAT WORDING PARTNERS EXPECT TO SEE

### ✅ GOOD Examples (Use These):

**About Page:**
> "TravelSearch is an independent flight comparison platform. We aggregate 
> flight data from multiple airlines and booking providers. When you click 
> to book, you'll be redirected to complete your purchase on the partner's 
> website. We may earn a commission from completed bookings."

**Search Results Disclaimer:**
> "Prices shown are provided by our partners and may change. When you select 
> a flight, you'll be directed to the booking provider to complete your purchase."

**Affiliate Disclosure:**
> "TravelSearch earns revenue through affiliate partnerships. When you book 
> through our links, we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. 
> This helps us provide our comparison service free of charge."

**Footer:**
> "TravelSearch is a flight comparison platform. We do not sell tickets. 
> All bookings are completed directly with airlines or booking providers."

### ❌ RED FLAGS (Avoid These):

**Problematic Claims:**
- ❌ "Guaranteed lowest prices"
- ❌ "Best deals always"
- ❌ "Official airline booking site"
- ❌ "Exclusive discounts"
- ❌ "We sell tickets"
- ❌ Hidden affiliate relationships
- ❌ Fake urgency ("Only 2 seats left!" without verification)

---

## 🚩 COMMON RED FLAGS THAT TRIGGER REJECTION

### Immediate Disqualifiers:
1. **No clear disclosure of affiliate relationships**
2. **Claiming to be an airline or official booking agent**
3. **Fake reviews or testimonials**
4. **Deceptive advertising (misleading ads/CTAs)**
5. **Copyright violations (unauthorized logo use)**
6. **Spam or low-quality content**
7. **Thin affiliate site (just referral links, no value)**
8. **Traffic fraud indicators**
9. **No contact information**
10. **Non-functional or broken site**

### Moderate Concerns:
1. **Poor mobile experience**
2. **Very slow load times**
3. **Excessive advertising**
4. **Aggressive pop-ups**
5. **No privacy policy**
6. **Unclear business model**
7. **Low-quality content**
8. **Minimal traffic**
9. **Brand-new domain (<3 months)**
10. **No social proof**

## 🎯 PARTNER APPLICATION STRATEGY

### Before Applying:
1. **Self-review** against this checklist (100% mandatory items)
2. **Test your site** on multiple devices and browsers
3. **Check analytics** (have some traffic data ready)
4. **Prepare screenshots** of key pages
5. **Draft your pitch** (why you're a good partner)

### Application Best Practices:
1. **Be honest** about traffic volume
2. **Highlight your niche** (if any)
3. **Explain your marketing strategy**
4. **Show professionalism** in communication
5. **Provide accurate information**
6. **Have a business plan** (even basic)

### After Applying:
1. **Respond promptly** to partner inquiries
2. **Be patient** (reviews take 7-14 days typically)
3. **Don't spam** with follow-ups
4. **If rejected**, ask for specific reasons
5. **Fix issues** and reapply after 30-90 days

## 📊 TRAFFIC & PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS

### Minimum Thresholds (Typical):
- **Monthly Visitors:** 1,000+ (some accept less for new sites)
- **Bounce Rate:** <70%
- **Avg. Session Duration:** >1 minute
- **Pages/Session:** >2
- **Traffic Sources:** Diverse (not 100% paid)

### Quality Signals:
- **Organic search traffic** (indicates SEO value)
- **Direct traffic** (brand recognition)
- **Returning visitors** (user loyalty)
- **Engaged users** (time on site, pages viewed)
- **Low bot traffic** (legitimate audience)
