Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD)

Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, with IATA code HYD, serves the city of Hyderabad. This comprehensive guide provides everything you need to know about flying through HYD.

Airport Overview

Located in Hyderabad, Rajiv Gandhi International Airport is a major aviation hub offering domestic and international flights.

The airport features modern terminals with excellent passenger facilities including duty-free shopping, restaurants, and lounges.
