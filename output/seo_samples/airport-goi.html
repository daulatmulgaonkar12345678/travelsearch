Goa International Airport (GOI)

Goa International Airport, with IATA code GOI, serves the city of Goa. This comprehensive guide provides everything you need to know about flying through GOI.

Airport Overview

Located in Goa, Goa International Airport is a major aviation hub offering domestic and international flights.

Terminal Information

The airport features modern terminals with excellent passenger facilities including duty-free shopping, restaurants, and lounges.

Facilities at GOI
