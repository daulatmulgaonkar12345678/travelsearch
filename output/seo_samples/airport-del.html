Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL)

Indira Gandhi International Airport, with IATA code DEL, serves the city of New Delhi. This comprehensive guide provides everything you need to know about flying through DEL.

Airport Overview

Located in New Delhi, Indira Gandhi International Airport is a major aviation hub offering domestic and international flights.

Terminal Information

The airport features modern terminals with excellent passenger facilities including duty-free shopping, restaurants, and lounges.

  • Metro/local train connections
  • Private car parking facilities

Facilities at DEL
